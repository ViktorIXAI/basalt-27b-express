### 1. Storage of Attention State

The project supports different KV cache formats depending on the model architecture, with specific byte costs per position:

**Qwen3.8-27B (Dense Model)**
*   **Formats:** `fp16` (default) and `int8`.
*   **Size:**
    *   `fp16`: **64 KiB** per position (across 16 attention layers).
    *   `int8`: **33 KiB** per position (int8 codes with an fp16 scale per 64 values).
*   **Capacity Example:** On a 32 GB card, one slot holds about **190K positions** in `fp16` and the full **262,144** positions in `int8`.

**DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash (Mixture-of-Experts)**
*   **Formats:** `fp8` (default) and `fp16`.
*   **Size:**
    *   `fp8`: **920 bytes** per position.
    *   `fp16`: **3,200 bytes** per position.
*   **Capacity Example:** A 1M context takes **0.97 GiB** in `fp8` versus **3.20 GiB** in `fp16`.

**Qwen3.8-Flash-Next (Mixture-of-Experts)**
*   **Formats:** `fp16` (default), `int8`, `q4_0`, and `k8v4`.
*   **Size:**
    *   `int8`: **1,056 bytes** per cell.
    *   `k8v4`: **816 bytes** per cell (K is int8, V is 4-bit).
    *   *Note: The text does not provide explicit byte counts for `fp16` or `q4_0` for this specific model, but notes `int8` halves VRAM compared to `fp16`.*
