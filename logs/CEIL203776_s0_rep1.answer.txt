Based on the documentation provided, here is the comparison of how attention state (KV cache) is stored and the associated decoding costs when that state exceeds GPU memory.

### 1. Storage of Attention State

The project stores attention state (Key/Value caches) in different formats depending on the model architecture, with specific byte costs per position:

**Qwen3.8-27B (Dense Model)**
*   **Formats:** `fp16` (default) or `int8`.
*   **Byte Cost:**
    *   `fp16`: **64 KiB** per position (across 16 attention layers).
    *   `int8`: **33 KiB** per position (int8 codes with an fp16 scale per 64 values).
*   **Capacity Example:** On a 32 GB card, one slot holds about **190K positions** in `fp16` and the full **262,144** positions in `int8`.

**DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash (Mixture-of-Experts)**
*   **Formats:** `fp8` (default) or `fp16`.
